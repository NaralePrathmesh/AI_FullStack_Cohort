AI Tools

ChatGPT

ChatGPT is an AI chatbot developed by OpenAI that can understand and generate human-like text. It is used for:

1.Answering questions and explaining concepts
2.Writing emails, documents, and content
3.Coding help and debugging
4.Learning and interview preparation
5.Data analysis and problem-solving

It is based on Large Language Models (LLMs) and can work with text, images, files, and other inputs depending on the version.

Claude

Claude is an AI assistant developed by Anthropic. It is designed to be helpful, safe, and good at understanding long documents.

It is used for:

1.Writing and editing content
2.Summarizing large documents
3.Coding assistance
4.Research and analysis
5.Business and professional tasks

Claude focuses strongly on long-context understanding, meaning it can process large amounts of information in a single conversation.
