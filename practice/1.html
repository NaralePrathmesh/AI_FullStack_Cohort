Lord Vishnu

Lord Vishnu is one of the three principal deities (Trimurti) in Hinduism. He is known as the Preserver and Protector of the universe, responsible for maintaining balance and righteousness (dharma). Short Information Role: Preserver of the universe. Consort: Goddess Lakshmi

Abode: Vaikuntha Mount (Vahana): Garuda Symbol: Rests on the serpent Shesha in the cosmic ocean. Four Hands Hold: Shankha (Conch): Symbolizes the sacred sound "Om". Chakra (Sudarshana): Represents protection and justice. Gada (Mace): Symbolizes strength and power. Padma (Lotus): Represents purity and spiritual growth.
